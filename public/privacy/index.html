Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective date: November 2026 · Package ID: app.tatani.android

1. Data We Collect

Tatani collects only the minimum data required to synchronize your Japanese language study across devices:

  • Account credentials (email address and authentication identifier via Supabase Auth).
  • Vocabulary study state, spaced-repetition intervals, quiz history, and selected anime library entries.
  • Optional push notification device tokens if flashcard reminders are explicitly enabled.

2. How We Use & Protect Data

All network communication is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS. Study progress is stored locally on-device for instant offline review and synchronized to your personal database solely to schedule flashcard reviews and personalize episode vocabulary. Tatani does not sell personal data, display advertisements, or share user data with third-party data brokers.

3. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your study history, anime library, notification tokens, and account progress data at any time directly inside the app:

  • Android app (app.tatani.android): Open Library → Account & Privacy (top-right icon) → Delete my study & account data and confirm permanent deletion.
  • Web app: Sign in at Settings and select Reset study data.
